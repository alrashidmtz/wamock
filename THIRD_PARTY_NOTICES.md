## libphonenumber

`src/core/national-lengths.ts` (and its compiled form in `dist/`) contains
the national number lengths of fixed-line and mobile numbers per calling
code, derived from `resources/PhoneNumberMetadata.xml` of libphonenumber
v9.0.41.
